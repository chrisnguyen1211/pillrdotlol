# pillr 1.2.2

Speak your reply, and reach every session.

- **Hold fn to speak.** In the reply field, or over a done card, hold fn and say your reply. It listens in the language of the keyboard you are using.
- **Replies reach sessions that are not on screen.** A Claude app or Codex app session that is not open is opened by its own link first. Anything already typed in its box is set aside and put back after.
- **More terminals.** Replies and focus work in tmux, Zellij, kitty and WezTerm, and a done card brings the right Ghostty tab or editor window forward.
- **Fewer false cards.** A permission Antigravity runs on its own no longer flashes a card, a session is said to wait only once it still waits, asks show without their tags, and a session file left by a crash no longer takes a card off the notch.
- **A truer dashboard.** A session left busy when pillr quit or the Mac slept no longer counts as work, a prompt pillr let through is not counted as your answer, and the streak counts past 61 days.
- **Lighter when Settings is closed.** The dashboard stops drawing and reading git once Settings is closed or hidden.
